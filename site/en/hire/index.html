How I work

The agent writes most of the code; I decide what gets published and read everything before it is published. Every claim on this site is mine. Some of my commits carry a co-author line for Claude, far from all of them, so the history shows the split only in part.

My setup (self-reported): a CLAUDE.md with working rules; subagents for search, implementation, tests and review; a guard hook in front of publishing, sending and payments — a tripwire, not a cage: in my own probe it missed most bypass attempts. Before any number leaves a project, a counter-check runs whose only job is to find the error. Separately, team-skills-kit, an open template for teams that maintain Claude Code skills together (test cases, review, a version rule and CI), is in preparation and not yet published.
