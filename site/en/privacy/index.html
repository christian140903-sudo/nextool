When you visit this website

This website is static. It loads no scripts, fonts or content from third-party servers, uses no analytics or statistics services and sets no cookies itself.

It is delivered through Cloudflare Pages (Cloudflare, Inc., USA). To deliver the page and protect it against attacks, Cloudflare processes technically necessary connection data: IP address, time, requested address and browser identification. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in a secure, working website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

If traffic looks suspicious, Cloudflare may show a security check and then set the cookie cf_clearance. It only serves to prevent abuse; I do not set it myself and do not analyse it.

When you email me

I process your address and message in order to reply (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR for enquiries about a contract, otherwise (f)). Mail to hello@nextool.app passes through the email forwarding of the domain registrar Namecheap (USA) into my mailbox; data may be transferred to the USA in the process. I delete emails no later than twelve months after the exchange ends, unless a statutory retention duty applies.

Links to other websites

Links to GitHub, npm and other sites only take you there when you click them; from then on, that provider's privacy notice applies. This website embeds no content from third parties.

Your rights
