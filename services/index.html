How It Works

Idea to Deployed in 4 Steps

No calls. No meetings. No scope creep. One email starts the process.

1

Email Your Idea

Describe what you need in plain English. A paragraph is enough. Attach screenshots or examples if you have them.

2

Agree on the Scope

Define the deliverables, acceptance criteria, ownership, timeline, dependencies, and price before implementation begins.

3

Review Working Evidence

Inspect the implementation through a repository, test output, package, staging build, or another artifact appropriate to the project.

4

Accept and Hand Off

Verify the agreed criteria, resolve documented gaps, and complete deployment, documentation, and source handoff.
