February 2026, generated by agents, no longer online.

What used to be here

From February to September 2026 this address served different content: a collection of small browser tools, AI-generated guide articles, shop prototypes, older product pages and a research side project. Almost all of it was generated by agents, much of it unchecked.

Why it is no longer online

  • Much of it was unchecked; some of the tools failed to load.
  • Some of it promised more than it could back up: numbers without a source, checkout flows without a product.
  • A few sample pages showed invented clients without labelling them as invented. They are deleted from the website and do not redirect anywhere.
  • Many pages loaded fonts from third-party servers.

I now only show what I can back up. Earlier experiments do not meet my current standards.
